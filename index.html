How it works

Three steps from unsure to decided

1

Take the assessment

Answer 13 questions about your subjects, interests and strengths. Your answers are saved on the device as you go, so you can stop and come back.

2

Read the rules

Understand how the selection works — the five main choices, the two alternatives, programme requirements and the Day or Boarding option for each school.

3

Build and check

Rank your schools, attach programmes, then review everything on one screen before you fill the official form.

Install Journa and keep it on your phone

Journa is a Progressive Web App. Install it from the browser menu or the button below, then open it like a normal app — full screen, no address bar.

Your assessment answers and school choices are stored on this device, so they survive an offline session and stay put when you update.
