Your Privacy Matters

At JOURNA, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

We do NOT collect or store any personal information. All assessment answers and career results are saved locally in your browser only. No data is sent to any server.

Local Storage

Your assessment answers and career matches are stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser history.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. The only storage used is for saving your assessment progress locally.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts and Font Awesome icons, which may collect anonymized usage data as per their privacy policies.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no information to access, modify, or delete. All your data stays on your device.
